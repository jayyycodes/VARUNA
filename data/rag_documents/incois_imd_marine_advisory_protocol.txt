INDIAN NATIONAL CENTRE FOR OCEAN INFORMATION SERVICES (INCOIS) &
INDIA METEOROLOGICAL DEPARTMENT (IMD), MINISTRY OF EARTH SCIENCES
STANDARD OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES — OCEAN STATE FORECAST & FISHERMEN ADVISORIES

1. Wave Height Safety Thresholds for Small Craft & Artisanal Vessels:
- Safe (Green): Significant wave height less than 1.5 metres. Normal fishing operations permitted.
- Caution (Yellow): Significant wave height between 1.5 and 2.5 metres. Moderate seas, small craft advised to operate close to coast and maintain radio watch.
- Unsafe / Warning (Red): Significant wave height exceeding 2.5 metres, or wind speeds exceeding 40 km/h (22 knots). Total suspension of small-craft fishing operations advised.

2. High Wave Alerts & Swell Surge:
INCOIS issues Swell Surge / Kallakkadal warnings when distant southern ocean swells travel northwards causing sudden coastal flooding without local winds. During Swell Surge alerts, beaching of boats and nearshore operations are suspended.

3. Cyclone and Squall Warnings:
When squally wind speed is expected to reach 45-55 km/h gusting to 65 km/h, fishermen are explicitly advised not to venture into deep sea. Vessels already at sea are instructed to return to nearest harbour or safe anchorage immediately.

4. Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) Advisory Interpretation:
- PFZ advisories delineate sea surface temperature (SST) thermal fronts and chlorophyll concentration zones derived from satellite sensors (Oceansat-3 OCM, MODIS).
- A valid PFZ advisory does NOT confer weather safety. Even if a PFZ zone shows high productivity score, vessel masters must prioritize Ocean State Forecast safety verdicts before departure.
