GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
REVENUE AND FORESTS DEPARTMENT / FISHERIES
THE MAHARASHTRA MARINE FISHING REGULATION ACT, 1981
(MAHARASHTRA ACT NO. LIV OF 1981)

Section 4: Regulation of Fishing in Specified Areas
(1) The State Government may, having regard to the need to protect the interests of different sections of persons engaged in fishing, conserve fish, and regulate fishing on a scientific basis, by order notified in the Official Gazette, regulate, restrict or prohibit in any specified area:
(a) the fishing by such class or classes of fishing vessels;
(b) the number of fishing vessels which may be used for fishing;
(c) the catching of such species of fish and for such period;
(d) the use of such fishing gear as may be specified.

(2) Prohibition of Mechanized Vessels in Inshore Waters:
No mechanized fishing vessel (including trawlers and motorized craft above prescribed horsepower) shall engage in fishing operations within the territorial waters measured from the baseline up to a distance of 5 nautical miles (approx 9.26 km) seaward.
The inshore zone within 5 nautical miles from the high water mark / baseline along the coast of Maharashtra is strictly reserved for non-mechanized traditional fishermen operating non-motorized wooden canoes, dinghies, and traditional gillnet crafts.

(3) Restrictions on Purse Seine Gear:
The operation of purse seine gear by mechanized fishing vessels is strictly prohibited within the territorial waters of Maharashtra up to 12 nautical miles from the shore, except as regulated by special notification. Trawling operations within the 5 nautical mile limit shall attract immediate seizure of the fishing craft and impounding of the catch.

Section 8: Licensing and Registration of Fishing Vessels
Every mechanized fishing vessel operating off the Maharashtra coast must possess a valid vessel registration certificate under Section 9 and a fishing licence issued by the Authorized Officer (Assistant Commissioner of Fisheries). Unregistered vessels or vessels lacking valid biometric identity cards for the crew shall not be permitted to venture into sea from any landing centre or fishing harbour.
