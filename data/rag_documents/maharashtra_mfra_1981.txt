AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE REGULATION OF FISHING BY FISHING VESSELS
IN THE SEA ALONG THE COAST LINE OF THE STATE OF MAHARASHTRA.

WHEREAS it is expedient to provide for the regulation of fishing by fishing vessels
in the sea along the coast line of the State of Maharashtra and for matters connected
therewith or incidental thereto; It is hereby enacted in the Thirty-second Year of the
Republic of India as follows:—

CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY

1. Short title, extent and commencement.—
(1) This Act may be called the Maharashtra Marine Fishing Regulation Act, 1981.
(2) It extends to the whole of the State of Maharashtra (including the territorial waters).
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the State Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.

2. Definitions.— In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—
(a) "Adjudicating Officer" means the Tahsildar having jurisdiction, and includes any
other officer of the Revenue Department, not below the rank of Tahsildar, authorised
by the State Government to exercise the powers conferred by this Act.
(b) "Appellate Board" means an Appellate Board constituted under section 18.
(c) "Enforcement Officer" means the Assistant Fisheries Development Officer having
jurisdiction, and includes any other officer of the Directorate of Fisheries not below
that rank, authorised to exercise powers under this Act.
(d) "fishing vessel" means a boat or ship, whether or not fitted with mechanical means
of propulsion, engaged in sea fishing for profit, and includes a country craft and a canoe.
(e) "Licensing Officer" means the Assistant Fisheries Development Officer having
jurisdiction, and includes any other officer of the Directorate of Fisheries authorised
to exercise powers under this Act.
(f) "Port" means the space within such limits as may be defined by the State
Government by notification, for the purpose of this Act.
(g) "prescribed" means prescribed by rules made under this Act.
(h) "registered fishing vessel" means a fishing vessel registered under section 9.
(i) "Registration Officer" means the officer in the State Port Organisation authorised
to exercise powers under this Act.
(j) "specified area" means such area in the sea along the entire coast line of the
State, but not beyond territorial waters, as may be specified by the State Government.
(k) "State" means the State of Maharashtra, and includes the territorial waters
along the entire coast line of that State.

3. Constitution of Advisory Committees.—
(1) The State Government may by order constitute an Advisory Committee for each
coastal District, with the District Magistrate as Chairman and the Assistant Director
of Fisheries of the District as member-secretary, with representatives of the State
Port Department and the Police Department as members. The Chairman may co-opt
representatives of other Government Departments and of fishermen and trade. The
Committee shall recommend to the State Government the regulations to be enforced
under Chapter II.
(2) The Committee shall advise the State Government on:
  (i) reservation of specified areas of the sea for fishing by vessels of a specified type;
  (ii) prohibition of vessels of specified type from fishing in any specified area;
  (iii) laying down maximum number of fishing vessels allowed in a specified area;
  (iv) laying down the maximum number of fishing vessels to be registered at each port;
  (v) regulation or prohibition of catching specified species of fish in specified areas;
  (vi) regulation or prohibition of specified fishing gear in specified areas;
  (vii) prescribing timings for fishing operations where necessary;
  (viii) any other matter facilitating effective enforcement of the Act.

CHAPTER II — REGULATION OF FISHING

4. Power to regulate, restrict or prohibit certain fishing activities within specified area.—
(1) The State Government may, having regard to the matters in sub-section (2) and
after consultation with the Advisory Committee, by order notified in the Official
Gazette, regulate, restrict or prohibit:
  (a) fishing in any specified area by specified classes of fishing vessels;
  (b) the number of fishing vessels which may be used for fishing in any specified area;
  (c) the catching in any specified area of specified species of fish for a specified period;
  (d) the use of specified fishing gear in any specified area.
(2) In making an order under sub-section (1), the State Government shall have
regard to:
  (a) the need to protect interests of different sections of persons engaged in fishing,
      particularly those using traditional fishing craft such as country craft or canoe;
  (b) the need to conserve fish and regulate fishing on a scientific basis;
  (c) the need to maintain law and order in the sea;
  (d) any other prescribed matter.

5. Prohibition of use of fishing vessels in contravention of any order made under
section 4.— No owner or master of a fishing vessel shall use it for fishing in any
manner which contravenes an order made under section 4. (Provisos protect passage
through specified areas and protect traditional fishing gear from damage.)

6. Licensing of fishing vessels.— Owners may apply to the Licensing Officer for a
licence to use a fishing vessel in a specified area. Licences are valid for three years,
renewable for similar periods.

7. Prohibition of using fishing vessels which are not licensed.— No person shall carry
on fishing in a specified area using an unlicensed fishing vessel after commencement
of this Act (with a grace period for vessels already in use).

8. Cancellation, suspension and amendment of licences.— The Licensing Officer may
suspend, cancel, vary, or amend a licence after giving the holder a reasonable
opportunity to be heard, for misrepresentation or failure to comply with conditions.

9. Registration of vessels.— Every owner of a vessel used for fishing and kept in the
State shall register the vessel; a Certificate of Registration is issued; vessels must
carry a registration mark.

10. Information to be given to Registration Officer about movement of fishing vessel.—
Owners must declare a base port and seek permission to shift operating base to
another port.

11. Returns to be made by owners of registered fishing vessels.— Owners must furnish
prescribed returns; the Registration Officer may inspect vessels to verify accuracy.

12. Finality of orders under sections 6, 8 and 9.— Decisions on licensing/registration
are final, subject to appeal under section 13.

13. Appeals against orders refusing grant of licence or registration, etc.— Appeals lie
to the Chief Ports Officer of the State Port Organisation within thirty days.

CHAPTER III — PENALTIES

14. Power to enter and search fishing vessel.— An Enforcement Officer with reason to
believe a vessel is being used in contravention of the Act may enter, search, impound
the vessel, and seize fish found in it.

15. Disposal of seized fish.— The Enforcement Officer keeps impounded vessels as
prescribed; seized fish may be disposed of with proceeds deposited with the
Adjudicating Officer.

16. Adjudication.— The Adjudicating Officer holds an inquiry into reported
contraventions after giving all parties a reasonable opportunity to be heard.

17. Imposition of penalty.— On finding a person guilty, the Adjudicating Officer may
impose a penalty (up to five thousand rupees, or five times the value of fish involved
if higher), and may direct cancellation/suspension of registration or licence, or
forfeiture of the vessel or fish to the State Government (subject to a due-care proviso
for the owner).

18. Constitution of Appellate Board and appeals to Appellate Board.— District
Magistrates constitute Appellate Boards; appeals against Adjudicating Officer orders
lie within thirty days (extendable to sixty days for sufficient cause), subject to deposit
of the penalty amount (waivable on hardship grounds).

19. Revision by Appellate Board.— The Appellate Board may call for and review records
of orders against which no appeal was filed.

20. Power of Adjudicating Officer and the Appellate Board in relation to holding
inquiry.— They have Civil Court powers under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
(summoning witnesses, requiring document production, receiving affidavit evidence,
etc.), and are deemed Civil Courts for certain Criminal Procedure Code purposes.

21. General provision for punishment of offences.— Contravention is punishable with
fine up to one thousand rupees, plus up to fifty rupees per day of continuing
contravention after first conviction.

22. Offences by companies.— Persons in charge of a company at the time of an offence,
and the company itself, are deemed guilty (with a due-diligence defense); directors/
managers/officers are also liable if the offence is attributable to their neglect or consent.

CHAPTER IV — MISCELLANEOUS

23. Exemptions.— The Act does not apply to government survey vessels; the State
Government may exempt classes of fishing vessels from all or part of the Act if in
the public interest.

24. Protection of action taken in good faith.— No suit or legal proceeding lies against
the Government or officers for anything done in good faith under this Act.

25. Power to make rules.— The State Government may make rules to carry out the
purposes of this Act, subject to prior publication and legislative review (thirty days
before both Houses).

26. Saving.— This Act does not apply to fishing and fisheries beyond territorial waters.
