GOVERNMENT OF TAMIL NADU
ANIMAL HUSBANDRY, DAIRYING AND FISHERIES DEPARTMENT
THE TAMIL NADU MARINE FISHING REGULATION ACT, 1983
(TAMIL NADU ACT NO. VIII OF 1983)

Section 5: Regulation of Inshore Fishing and Demarcation of Zones
(1) Inshore waters up to a distance of 3 nautical miles (approx 5.56 km) from the shoreline along the entire coast of Tamil Nadu are strictly reserved for fishing exclusively by traditional, non-mechanized crafts including kattumarams, country craft, vallams, and fiber-reinforced plastic (FRP) canoes using non-motorized gears.

(2) Mechanized fishing boats, commercial bottom trawlers, and pair trawlers are prohibited from carrying out any fishing activity within the 3 nautical mile inshore zone. Mechanized fishing vessels may operate only beyond 3 nautical miles from the coast.

Section 7: Three-Day / Four-Day Rule for Mechanized Trawlers
To avoid conflict between traditional and mechanized fishermen:
(a) Mechanized trawlers shall venture into sea from designated fishing harbours only on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays, and must return within 24 hours (known as the 3-day / 4-day fishing schedule).
(b) Traditional crafts have unfettered round-the-clock access to inshore waters.

Section 17: Penalties and Forfeiture
Any mechanized vessel found operating within 3 nautical miles, or utilizing pair trawls or banned destructive mesh sizes under 25 mm, shall be liable to suspension of registration, confiscation of the vessel, and penalties up to Rs. 25,000 for the first offense and Rs. 50,000 for subsequent offenses.
