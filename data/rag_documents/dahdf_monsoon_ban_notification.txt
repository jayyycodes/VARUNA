MINISTRY OF FISHERIES, ANIMAL HUSBANDRY AND DAIRYING
DEPARTMENT OF FISHERIES (DAHDF), GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
NOTIFICATION — UNIFORM FISHING BAN IN THE INDIAN EXCLUSIVE ECONOMIC ZONE (EEZ)

Subject: Annual closed fishing season / uniform monsoon fishing ban for conservation of marine fishery resources and breeding of fish.

1. West Coast (Arabian Sea):
- Geographic Scope: The entire Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) of India along the western maritime states including Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, and the Union Territories of Daman & Diu and Lakshadweep.
- Period of Ban: Total prohibition on fishing by all mechanized and motorized fishing vessels for a period of 61 days from 1st June to 31st July annually during the South-West Monsoon season.
- Exceptions: Non-motorized traditional country crafts and non-mechanized traditional canoes are exempted from the ban to sustain the livelihood of artisanal coastal fishermen.

2. East Coast (Bay of Bengal):
- Geographic Scope: The entire Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) of India along the eastern maritime states including West Bengal, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and the Union Territories of Puducherry and Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
- Period of Ban: Total prohibition on fishing by all mechanized and motorized fishing vessels for a period of 61 days from 15th April to 14th June annually during the North-East / pre-monsoon breeding season.
- Exceptions: Non-motorized traditional craft are exempted.

3. Purpose and Scientific Rationale:
The monsoon closed season coincides with the primary breeding and spawning period of commercially important marine species such as Indian mackerel, oil sardine, silver pomfret, and penaeid prawns. Ceasing mechanized bottom trawling during this period prevents destruction of egg beds, allows juvenile fish recruitment, and prevents stock collapse.

4. Enforcement:
State Fisheries Departments, Indian Coast Guard, and Coastal Security Police are mandated to enforce the ban strictly. Violation of the uniform ban attracts seizure of vessel, cancellation of diesel subsidy, and forfeiture of catch.
